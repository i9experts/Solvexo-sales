## Do not say yet (needs confirmation)

| Claim | Why it's on hold |
|---|---|
| "Free" on its own, or "free forever" | It's a **7-day** free trial, then paid. Always say "7 days free". |
| "Account ready within 24 hours" | The flyer says this, but the website says sellers go live immediately. Use "live straight away" and fix the flyer. |
| "AI analytics" / "AI grows your business" | The website says AI **suggests product titles and descriptions**. Analytics are real reports, not AI. The flyer's "real AI analytics" overstates this. |
| Mobile POS app | The For Sellers page says "mobile POS", but the About page says a **native POS app is on the roadmap**. Demo POS in the browser only, until confirmed. |
| Custom domain (your own .com) | Listed on plans, but the About page says custom domains are **on the roadmap**. Confirm before promising. |
| "Get paid within 2 business days, every time" | On the For Sellers page, but payouts for Pakistani sellers are unconfirmed |
| "Trusted by 76+ sellers" | On the For Sellers page; confirm the number is current and real |
| "Verified sellers" | The FAQ says sellers are verified, but the About page says there's no review queue. These contradict each other. |
| Any comparison with Daraz, Shopify or other platforms by name | Needs a fact-checked comparison first |

## Never say

- Guaranteed sales, income or growth
- "Best", "No. 1" or "cheapest" without proof
- Anything about a customer's results without their written permission
- Sample figures from the website or app mockups (e.g. "$18.4k revenue") as if they were real results
