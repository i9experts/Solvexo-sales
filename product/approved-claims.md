## Do not say yet (needs confirmation)

| Claim | Why it's on hold |
|---|---|
| "Free" / "Start Selling Free" in your own words | The website button says it, but plan details (what's free, for how long, fees) aren't confirmed |
| "3-minute setup" / "Account ready within 24 hours" | On the flyer, but conflicts with "no approval queue". Confirm which is accurate. |
| Any price, discount or fee | No approved pricing |
| Payment methods and payout timing | Not confirmed for Pakistani sellers |
| Number of sellers, sales volume, ratings | Figures in app screenshots are demo/early data, not marketing claims |
| "AI does X for you" (specific features) | Confirm what the AI analytics actually shows |
| Comparisons with Daraz, Shopify or other platforms by name | Needs fact-checked comparison first |

## Never say

- Guaranteed sales, income or growth
- "Best", "No. 1" or "cheapest" without proof
- Anything about a customer's results without their written permission
