# Pricing

**Source:** published on [solvexo.store/pricing](https://www.solvexo.store/pricing), copied **21 Nov 2026**. The website is the source of truth. Check it before quoting, because the launch offer can change.

## Launch offer (on the website now)

- **40% off every plan** (prices below already include it)
- **7-day free trial**, no credit card needed
- Then **US$1 a month for the first 3 months** (Basic, Grow, Advanced), then the plan price
- Cancel or change plan anytime

**Confirm:** when does the launch offer end? Don't say "limited time" until there is a real end date.

## Plans (US dollars, per month)

| Plan | Normal | Launch price (monthly) | Paid yearly | For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | $10 | **$6** | $5 | Starting out: up to 25 products, 1 extra staff login, 1 location, 3 currencies |
| **Grow** (most popular) | $49 | **$29** | $23.50 | Growing shop: unlimited products, 5 staff, 3 locations, loyalty points, wholesale, digital memberships, courses, priority support |
| **Advanced** | $99 | **$59** | $47.50 | Several branches: 15 staff, 10 locations, live courier rates, Search Console, API |

## What sellers pay on each sale

| Payment type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Cash on delivery, bank transfer, in-store sales | **0%** |
| Card payments through Solvexo Payments | **0% commission** (normal card processing fee only) |
| Seller's own gateway (e.g. their own Stripe or PayPal) | 2% Basic · 1% Grow · 0.6% Advanced · 0.2% Enterprise |

## Which plan to suggest

| Seller says | Suggest |
|---|---|
| "I'm just starting, a few products" | Basic |
| "My shop is growing and I have staff" | Grow |
| "I have several branches or a big brand" | Advanced |
