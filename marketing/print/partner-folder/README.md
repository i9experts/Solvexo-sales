# Partner Presentation Folder (D1): A4 pocket folder

Holds the partnership pack for partner meetings (Lahore partner first): MOU, Channel Partner Programme, sales sheet, flyer and a business card.

## Artwork (in `output/`, print file `Solvexo_Partner_Folder_PRINT_all_parts.pdf`)

| Page | Part | Size incl. 3 mm bleed | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Outside**: back · spine · front | 451 × 316 mm | Front: terracotta, logo, "Channel Partner Programme", **"Grow commerce in your city, together."**, "Partnership pack". Spine: "SOLVEXO · CHANNEL PARTNER PROGRAMME". Back: dark, platform statement (EN/UR), contact, Powered by i9 Experts, QR to solvexo.store |
| 2 | **Inside**: left · spine · right | 451 × 316 mm | Left: welcome, what's inside (MOU, Programme, sales sheet & flyer, contact), 4 values (in writing, paid on time, no interest, honest selling), write-in partnership contact. Right: the 4 tiers and the 4-step path (Meet → Agree → Pilot 90 days → Grow); bottom 100 mm is covered by the pocket |
| 3 | **Pocket** face | 226 × 106 mm | "Your partnership pack" + business-card slot (die-cut guide only, not printed) |

Panels are 220 × 310 mm (fits A4 with room), spine 5 mm (about 25 sheets). **Give these to the printer to place on their own standard A4 pocket-folder dieline** (single right-hand pocket, 100 mm deep, glued, business-card slits on the pocket).

- **No commission rates or prices** are printed: the folder has a long life and terms are still proposed. Rates live in the MOU/Programme inside.
- **Stock:** 350 gsm art card, matte laminate outside; spot UV on the front logo optional. 25 to start.
- Rebuild: `node render.js` (per-part PDFs + previews), then `pdfunite` as in the commit.
