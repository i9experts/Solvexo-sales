# Seller Setup Checklist Pad (E4): A4 tear-off pad

The rep's go-live form. One sheet per seller, ticked **together with the seller** during setup, so nothing is missed and the seller sees their store is really ready.

- **Header:** shop name, date, owner, WhatsApp, store link (`____.solvexo.store`), area/market, rep name, seller type.
- **6 sections with tick boxes + rep initials:** 1 Account (seller's own email/Google, can log in, trial plan) · 2 Store (name/link, theme) · 3 Payments (COD / bank transfer / card, store shows live) · 4 Products (**at least 5 real products** with photo, price, stock; variants) · 5 POS & staff (test sale, staff PINs, cheat sheet left) · 6 Share & handover (bio/WhatsApp status, tent card/decal, quick-start guide, rep's number saved, trial explained honestly).
- **Store counts as "live" only when sections 1–4 are done.** This matches the 30-day plan's definition.
- **Follow-ups:** Day 3 and Day 7 check-in dates, "pipeline tracker updated" box, store live date.
- **Consent (EN/UR):** seller agrees to be contacted on WhatsApp about their store. Needed before adding them to any WhatsApp follow-up.
- Seller and rep signatures.

## Print specs

- A4 (210 × 297 mm) + 3 mm bleed. **Pads of 50 sheets, glued at the top** (top 15 mm kept clear). 80–100 gsm uncoated so any pen writes well.
- Light ink use: terracotta accents only; prints fine on a 2-colour press.
- **Optional:** 2-part carbonless (NCR) pads: white copy for the seller, yellow copy for the rep.
- 3 pads to start (1 per field rep + spare).
- After each visit, the rep enters the sheet in `trackers/Solvexo_Seller_Pipeline_Tracker.xlsx` the same day. Store completed sheets securely: they contain sellers' personal details.

Rebuild: `node render.js` → `output/setup-checklist.pdf` + `output/preview.png`.
