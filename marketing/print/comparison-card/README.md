# Before / After Comparison Card (A3): A5 landscape, laminated

A **talking prop the rep keeps**, not a leave-behind. Hold it up at the counter: left side "this is your shop today", right side "this is your shop on Solvexo".

- **Front (for the shop owner):** TODAY: stock register with crossed-out counts, WhatsApp order chat, broken spreadsheet + 3 pains (EN/UR). WITH SOLVEXO: one dashboard (sales, orders, low stock, weekly chart, latest orders, "stock synced") + 3 gains (EN/UR). Sample data, labelled.
- **Back (for the rep):** 4 qualifying questions, the 4-step demo flow, 3 quick answers to common objections, the approved-claims reminder, and the closing line in English and Urdu.
- **Size:** A5 landscape (210 × 148 mm) + 3 mm bleed = 216 × 154 mm.
- **Stock:** 300 gsm, double-sided, **gloss or matte lamination both sides** (it gets handled all day). 20 to start: 2 per rep plus spares.
- No prices on the card; reps show the sales sheet for current prices.
- Rebuild: `node render.js` → `output/card.pdf` (page 1 front, page 2 back) + PNG previews.
