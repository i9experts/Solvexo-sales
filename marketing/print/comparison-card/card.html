Quick answers

“I already sell on Instagram.”Keep doing it. Put your store link in your bio, so every order lands in one place instead of scattered DMs.
“I’m not good with technology.”If you use WhatsApp, you can use this. Let’s add your first product together right now.
“How much? How do I get paid?”7 days free. Show the sales sheet for current prices. COD and bank transfer come straight to you; 0% commission on them.

Say only approved claims. No promises about sales or income, the mobile POS app or custom domains. Prices only from solvexo.store/pricing.

Closing line: “Your shop counter and your online store, one app, one stock count. Shall we put your first product live now? It’s 7 days free, no card needed.”
