STEP 1 · 2 MINUTES

Sign up free

Every new store gets a 7-day free trial with full access. No card needed to start.

  1. Scan the QR on the cover, or go to solvexo.store and tap Start Selling.
  2. Sign up with your email or Google.
  3. Enter the one-time code sent to your email.
  4. Choose a plan to try. You can change it later.

solvexo.store پر جائیں یا QR اسکین کریں۔ ای میل یا Google سے اکاؤنٹ بنائیں، اور ای میل پر آنے والا کوڈ ڈال دیں۔

STEP 2 · 3 MINUTES

Set up your store

  1. Follow the setup steps after you sign up.
  2. Enter your store name. This creates your link, e.g. yourname.solvexo.store.
  3. Pick a theme. Add your logo and colours whenever you like.

STEP 3 · 2 MINUTES

Turn on payments

  1. Open payment settings and turn on how you’ll be paid, e.g. cash on delivery or bank transfer (0% commission on both).

STEP 4 · 1 MINUTE PER PRODUCT

Add your products

  1. Go to Products and tap Add product.
  2. Add a photo, the name and the price.
  3. Enter the stock quantity. This one number is used by both your online store and your POS.
  4. Has sizes or colours? Add them as variants, each with its own stock.
  5. Save. The product appears in your store at once.

ہر پروڈکٹ کی تصویر، نام، قیمت اور اسٹاک ڈالیں۔ کم از کم 5 پروڈکٹس سے شروع کریں۔

STEP 5 · 5 MINUTES

Share your store link

Your link looks like yourname.solvexo.store. Put it everywhere your customers already are:

  1. Instagram / Facebook bio: paste the link so followers can order themselves.
  2. WhatsApp status and your WhatsApp Business profile.
  3. At the counter: your Solvexo team can give you a QR stand card for customers to scan.
  4. On every bag or parcel: “Reorder online: yourname.solvexo.store”.

اپنا اسٹور لنک انسٹاگرام بائیو، واٹس ایپ اسٹیٹس اور کاؤنٹر پر لگائیں، تاکہ کسٹمر خود آرڈر کر سکیں۔

Message existing customers once (only those who chat with you already): “We’re online now! Order any time: [your link]”.

STEP 6 · 1 MINUTE

Your first sale at the counter

  1. Open POS and log in with your staff PIN.
  2. Search the product and add it to the cart. Add a discount if needed.
  3. Take payment: cash (change is worked out for you), card, or split.
  4. Give the receipt. Stock drops by itself, online too.

POS کھولیں، PIN سے لاگ اِن کریں، پروڈکٹ تلاش کریں، پیمنٹ لیں اور رسید دیں۔ اسٹاک خود ہی کم ہو جائے گا۔
