# Seller Quick-start Guide (E1): A5 booklet, 8 pages, English + Urdu

Given to every new seller at sign-up (or left after the demo), so they can get from sign-up to first sale without calling the rep.

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cover: "Your store, live today", the 6 steps, QR to start |
| 2 | Step 0: checklist of what to have ready + what you'll have at the end |
| 3 | Step 1: Sign up (email/Google, one-time code, 7-day free trial, no card) |
| 4 | Step 2: Set up your store (name → link, theme) · Step 3: Turn on payments (COD/bank transfer, live straight away) |
| 5 | Step 4: Add products (photo, price, stock, variants) + photo tip |
| 6 | Step 5: Share your link (bio, WhatsApp, counter QR card, bags) |
| 7 | Step 6: First POS sale (PIN, search, payment, receipt) + daily routine |
| 8 | Help: rep contact (write-in), manuals QR (solvexo.store/manuals, EN/UR), email support, after the trial, first-week checklist |

- **Size:** A5 (148 × 210 mm) + 3 mm bleed. 8 pages, **saddle-stitched** (2 staples). The PDF is in reading order; the printer imposes it.
- **Stock:** cover and inside on 150–170 gsm matte (a self-cover booklet), or 250 gsm cover + 130 gsm inside. 200 to start.
- **QR codes:** cover → `solvexo.store/?utm_source=quickstart`; page 8 → `solvexo.store/manuals?utm_source=quickstart`.
- **Screens are illustrations** (labelled), not real screenshots.

## Check before printing (open items)

Button and menu names in the steps (*Start Selling*, *Products → Add product*, *payment settings*, *POS*, *staff PIN*, *start/end shift*) follow the website and product notes. **Have someone walk through the live app once with this guide** and correct any label that differs. Also confirm that sellers can turn on cash on delivery and bank transfer themselves.

Rebuild: `node render.js` → `output/quick-start-guide.pdf` + `output/page-N.png`.
