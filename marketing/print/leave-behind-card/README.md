# Demo Leave-behind Card (A4): business-card size, double-sided

Left with the shop owner after a visit or demo, to keep by the till.

- **Front:** "7 days free. No card needed." + one-line benefit + QR ("Scan to start") + terracotta strip with solvexo.store and "0% commission on COD & shop sales".
- **Back:** Urdu offer line, a write-in "Your Solvexo contact" (name + WhatsApp), Powered by Solvexo · i9 Experts, sales email. Light background so a pen writes clearly.
- **Size:** 3.5 × 2 in (88.9 × 50.8 mm) + 3 mm bleed = 94.9 × 56.8 mm. Text kept 4 mm inside the trim.
- **Stock:** 350 gsm matte card, double-sided. **Uncoated or matte on the back** so reps can write on it (gloss smudges). 1,000 to start.
- **QR:** `https://solvexo.store/?utm_source=card-a4&utm_medium=print` (about 22 mm). Test before printing.
- No prices on the card (long shelf life). The offer details live on the sales sheet and the website.
- Rebuild: `node render.js` → `output/card.pdf` (page 1 front, page 2 back) + PNG previews.
