POS cheat sheet · keep at the till

کاؤنٹر پر رکھیں

Start your shift

  1. Open POS.
  2. Enter your own PIN.
  3. Start shift. Enter opening cash if asked.

POS کھولیں، اپنا PIN ڈالیں، شفٹ شروع کریں۔

Make a sale

  1. Search the product by name.
  2. Tap it to add to cart. Change quantity if needed.
  3. Check the total, then tap Pay.

پروڈکٹ تلاش کریں، کارٹ میں ڈالیں، Pay دبائیں۔

Discount or coupon

  1. Tap Discount.
  2. Choose % or a fixed amount, or enter a coupon code.
  3. Only give discounts your manager allows.

ڈسکاؤنٹ یا کوپن کوڈ — صرف منیجر کی اجازت سے۔

Add the customer

  1. Tap Customer.
  2. Search by name or phone, or add a new one.
  3. Their purchases and points are saved.

کسٹمر کا نام یا فون نمبر تلاش کر کے سیل میں ڈالیں۔

Take payment

  1. Cash: enter the amount given. Change is shown.
  2. Card: choose card.
  3. Split: part cash, part card.

کیش، کارڈ، یا دونوں — بقایا خود حساب ہو جاتا ہے۔

Receipt

  1. After payment, print or send the receipt.
  2. Stock drops by itself, in the shop and online.
  3. Don’t change stock by hand after a POS sale.

رسید دیں۔ اسٹاک خود ہی کم ہو جاتا ہے۔

Hold a sale

  1. Customer still deciding? Tap Hold.
  2. Serve the next customer.
  3. Open held sales to finish it.

سیل ہولڈ کریں، اگلے کسٹمر کو دیکھیں، بعد میں مکمل کریں۔

End your shift

  1. Open the shift summary.
  2. Count the cash and compare.
  3. End shift and log out.

شفٹ سمری دیکھیں، کیش گنیں، شفٹ ختم کریں۔

Good habits · quick fixes · help

اچھی عادتیں اور مدد

Every day at the till

MorningStart your shift with your own PIN. Check new online orders.
Every saleRing up every sale on POS, even small ones, so stock stays right.
Online ordersCheck them a few times a day and pack them quickly.
EveningShift summary, count cash, end shift, log out.

ہر سیل POS پر کریں، چاہے چھوٹی ہو — تب ہی اسٹاک صحیح رہے گا۔

If something goes wrong

Added the wrong item?Remove it from the cart before you tap Pay.
Sale already completed by mistake?Don’t try to fix stock by hand. Tell your manager straight away.
Product not found?Check the spelling. If it isn’t in the list, ask your manager to add it.
Internet is down?Note sales on paper and enter them as soon as you’re back online.

غلطی ہو جائے تو فوراً منیجر کو بتائیں۔

Keep it safe · get help

Never share your PIN. Every sale is saved under your name.
Log out when you leave the counter.
