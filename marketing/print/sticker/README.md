# "Powered by Solvexo" sticker (B3)

Small round sticker for live sellers' counters, POS screens, packing tables and the team's laptops. Same for everyone, so no personalisation.

## Two styles

- **Ring:** terracotta badge, "POWERED BY" on the top arc, "SOLVEXO.STORE" on the bottom arc, logo in the centre. Stands out on glass, counters and laptops.
- **Clean:** white with a terracotta ring, logo, "POWERED BY" and the Solvexo wordmark. Quieter, for sellers who want something discreet on a POS screen or till.

## Files (`output/`)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `sticker_single_ring_clean_cutline.pdf` | Page 1 ring · page 2 clean · page 3 cut line. Send this to a sticker printer who lays out their own sheets |
| `A4_sheet_ring.pdf`, `A4_sheet_clean.pdf` | 12 stickers per A4 with magenta cut circles (the magenta lines are a guide and must not be printed) |
| `preview_ring.png`, `preview_clean.png` | Previews for approval / WhatsApp |

Rebuild with `node render.js`.

## Print specs

- **Size:** 2 in (50.8 mm) circle, 2 mm bleed. Kiss-cut on a sheet, or die-cut singles.
- **Material:** white gloss vinyl with matte or gloss laminate (water and scratch resistant). For glass, the same works stuck on the outside.
- **Quantity:** 200 to start (about 150 ring, 50 clean is a good split).
- Ask the printer for a colour proof of the terracotta (`#D97757`).
