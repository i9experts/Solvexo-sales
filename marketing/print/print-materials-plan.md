# Solvexo Printed Marketing & Brand Materials: Master List

Written 22 Nov 2026. Matched to the 30-day sales plan (`sales/plan/`), the partner programme (`partners/`) and the approved claims list (`product/approved-claims.md`).

**Priority:** **P1** = needed in week 1 of the 30-day plan · **P2** = month 2 · **P3** = once there are live sellers and results to show.
**Quantities** are starting suggestions for the 30-day plan (2 field reps, 1 online rep, 10 founding sellers, 1 Lahore partner). Get print quotes before ordering; no prices are assumed here.

---

## Rules for every printed piece

1. **Approved claims only.** Use only claims on `product/approved-claims.md`. Never print "AI analytics", "account ready within 24 hours", "mobile POS app" or "custom domain" until confirmed.
2. **Prices only on short-life items.** The launch offer (40% off, US$1 × 3 months) will change. Put prices only on items printed in small runs, and add "Offer valid at time of printing, see solvexo.store". Long-life items (standees, plaques, merch) carry no prices.
3. **A tracked QR code on every piece.** Each item gets its own QR code with a tracking tag, e.g. `solvexo.store/?utm_source=flyer&utm_medium=print&utm_campaign=karachi-blitz`, so you can see which piece brings sign-ups. Test every QR before printing.
4. **English + Urdu.** Seller-facing pieces in both. Urdu written fresh, not translated (see the pitch file).
5. **Brand basics:** logo from `brand/source/Solvexo_brand_logo.pdf` (vector), Terracotta `#D97757`, Light Coral `#F59373`, black, white. Tagline: *Create. Sell. Manage. Grow.*
6. **Print specs:** CMYK, 300 dpi, 3 mm bleed, text 5 mm inside the trim, export as PDF/X. Large formats (standees, banners) at 100–150 dpi at full size.
7. **Dignified visuals:** no identifiable customer photos without written permission; modest imagery.
