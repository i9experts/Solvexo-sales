# One-page Sales Sheet (A2): A4, single-sided

Handed to a shop owner **after a demo**, so they have the plans, prices and fees in writing (honest-selling rule: the seller sees the full offer before committing).

- **Source:** `sales-sheet.html` (uses the fonts in `../tent-card/fonts/`). Rebuild with `node render.js` → `output/sales-sheet.pdf` + `output/preview.png`.
- **Size:** A4 (210 × 297 mm) + 3 mm bleed = 216 × 303 mm. Text kept at least 9 mm from the sheet edge.
- **Stock:** 200 gsm matte art paper, single-sided. 300 to start.
- **QR:** `https://solvexo.store/?utm_source=salessheet-a2&utm_medium=print`. Test before printing.
- **Content source:** plans and fees copied from solvexo.store/pricing (Nov 2026) and `pricing/README.md`; features from `product/approved-claims.md`. Custom domains, mobile POS app and "AI analytics" are left out on purpose.
- **Rep fills in by hand:** name, WhatsApp, plan suggested, follow-up date.
- **Before every reprint:** check the pricing page. If the launch offer (40% off, US$1 × 3 months) has changed, update the plan prices, the struck-through prices and the offer box.
