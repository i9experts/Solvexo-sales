# Solvexo Sales

Sales and marketing material for **Solvexo**, a connected commerce platform for building an online store, selling in person with POS, managing orders and growing with AI analytics. Website: [solvexo.store](https://solvexo.store). Built by i9 Experts.

> **Create. Sell. Manage. Grow.**

## What's in here

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`brand/`](brand/) | Logos (PNG with transparent background and JPG on white), source PDF, launch video, colours and logo rules |
| [`marketing/`](marketing/) | Finished creative such as flyers and posters |
| [`product/`](product/) | What Solvexo does, who it is for, and the **approved claims list** (check it before you say anything to a prospect) |
| [`sales/plan/`](sales/plan/) | 30-day sales plan: goal, actions, budget, weekly targets |
| [`sales/pitch/`](sales/pitch/) | Elevator pitch and one-liners in English and Urdu |

| [`sales/outreach/`](sales/outreach/) | WhatsApp and follow-up message templates in English and Urdu |
| [`sales/demo/`](sales/demo/) | Live demo script |
| [`pricing/`](pricing/) | Pricing status. **Nothing is approved yet.** |
| [`partners/`](partners/) | Reseller / partner programme status. **Nothing is approved yet.** |
| [`trackers/`](trackers/) | Excel seller pipeline tracker with a dashboard |

## Ground rules for everyone selling Solvexo

1. **Only say what is on the approved claims list** in [`product/approved-claims.md`](product/approved-claims.md). If a prospect asks about something not on it, say you'll confirm and come back.
2. **Never quote a price, discount, commission or payout date** that isn't written in [`pricing/`](pricing/) as approved.
3. **Never commit real customer data to this repo.** Keep your filled-in lead tracker in your own drive. This repo holds the blank template only (see `.gitignore`).
4. Use the logo files in [`brand/logos/`](brand/logos/) as they are. Don't stretch, recolour or redraw them.
