## Part A: For management (internal)

### A1. Summary

You offered a Lahore company (a channel partner of Alibaba) **40% commission** and **Lahore as their region** in an informal discussion. Nothing is signed and they haven't seen a presentation yet.

**Recommendation:**

1. **Keep the 40%.** You said it, and keeping your word matters both in business and in Islam (Qur'an 5:1, "fulfil your contracts"). But define it in writing *before* it becomes an assumption. The definition matters more than the number:
   - 40% **of what**: net subscription fees actually received, excluding taxes, card processing fees and refunds
   - **for which work**: selling *plus* local onboarding, first-line support and PKR collection
   - **for how long** per seller
2. **Make the 40% the top reseller tier, not the starting point for everyone.** It's generous for a partner who only introduces sellers. It's fair for a partner who sells, onboards, supports and collects payment locally, because that partner removes cost and work from Solvexo.
3. **Lahore exclusivity should be earned, not given on day one.** Offer a **90-day pilot** as a non-exclusive Authorised Reseller. Lahore becomes an exclusive region only after agreed milestones are met, and stays exclusive only while they continue to be met.
4. **This partner can solve your biggest problem.** Solvexo charges in US$ by card, and many Pakistani shops can't pay that way. An authorised reseller can **bill sellers in PKR** and pay Solvexo in bulk. That alone makes the partnership worth 40%. Settle it first in the commercial schedule.
5. **Do your own checks on them too.** "Channel partner of Alibaba" is their claim. Ask for proof of registration, ownership and references before signing (section B12).

### A2. Does 40% make business sense?

The illustration below uses **published Solvexo prices** (US$, after the 40% launch discount) and an **assumed exchange rate of US$1 = PKR 280**. Check the current rate before showing any PKR figures.

| Plan | Seller pays (per month) | 40% partner share | Solvexo keeps (before its costs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | US$6 (≈ PKR 1,680) | US$2.40 (≈ PKR 672) | US$3.60 |
| Grow | US$29 (≈ PKR 8,120) | US$11.60 (≈ PKR 3,248) | US$17.40 |
| Advanced | US$59 (≈ PKR 16,520) | US$23.60 (≈ PKR 6,608) | US$35.40 |

**What this tells you:**

- On **Basic**, the partner earns about PKR 672 a month per seller. That's too little to cover a field visit, so the partner will naturally push **Grow**. That's fine, as long as they only sell Grow to sellers who need it (honest-selling rule, B9).
- Solvexo's 60% must still cover card processing, hosting, development and second-line support. **Calculate Solvexo's cost per seller per month** before signing. If that cost is more than about half of Basic's price, 40% on Basic loses money. *(This term requires management approval: Solvexo cost-per-seller figure.)*
- **The launch offer pays very little:** 40% of US$1 is US$0.40 a month for the first 3 months. A partner doing real field work will need an activation bonus, or they won't push during launch. *(Proposal: a one-time activation bonus per seller that goes live and stays paid for 2 full months. This term requires management approval: amount.)*

## Part B: The programme (partner-facing)

> *Proposed terms, subject to management approval.*

### B1. Why partner with Solvexo

Solvexo is one connected commerce platform. Sellers build an online store, sell in person with POS, and manage stock, orders, customers and analytics in one place, all from the same data.

- **A product sellers already need:** shops and social sellers want to be online without juggling five tools.
- **Low-risk to sell:** a 7-day free trial with no card needed, and 0% commission on cash on delivery and in-store sales.
- **Recurring income:** you earn on every month your sellers stay subscribed, not just once.
- **A real local role:** sellers want someone nearby to set them up and answer the phone. That's your value, and you get paid for it.

### B2. Partnership models

Every agreement states which model applies. They're different contracts and aren't mixed.

| Model | Who it suits | What you do | Who the seller's contract is with | Who bills the seller |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Referral Agent** | Individuals: freelancers, consultants, trainers | Introduce sellers using your partner link or code | Solvexo | Solvexo |
| **2. Authorised Reseller** | Registered businesses with a sales team | Sell, onboard, give first-line support | You (resale) or Solvexo (agency): stated in your schedule | You (in PKR) or Solvexo |
| **3. Service Partner** | Agencies, IT firms, photographers, designers | Paid setup work: store design, product upload, training | Seller contracts with you for services; Solvexo for software | Each bills its own part |
| **4. Regional Partner** | A Gold reseller who has met regional milestones | Everything in model 2, plus building the market in a defined region | As model 2 | As model 2 |

**How these map to Islamic contract forms** (for the Shariah reviewer to confirm, not a certification):
- **Referral Agent:** a known fee for a defined result (*juʿālah*), or agency for a fee (*wakālah bil-ujrah*).
- **Authorised Reseller, agency version:** *wakālah bil-ujrah*. You act for Solvexo and earn a known commission.
- **Authorised Reseller, resale version:** you buy subscription rights (a licence/service, not ownership of software) at a known price and resell them. The licence terms must allow this.
- **Service Partner:** hire of services (*ijārah*) with defined deliverables and price.

*Co-funded marketing: amount and rules require management approval.*
Tier thresholds (25 / 100 sellers) are **proposals**. Tiers are reviewed every quarter. A partner moves down only after a written warning and a 60-day chance to recover. Commission already earned is never taken back because of a tier change.

### B4. Commission and payment rules

1. **Commission base:** subscription fees **actually received** by Solvexo (or, in resale, the wholesale amount you pay), **excluding** sales tax/VAT, card processing fees, refunds and chargebacks.
2. **Earning event:** commission is earned when the seller's payment is received, not when they sign up or start a free trial.
3. **What's included:** new subscriptions, renewals, and upgrades by sellers attributed to you. Add-ons and services are treated separately in your schedule.
4. **Duration** *(Proposal)*: paid for as long as the seller stays subscribed and you continue to support them. If you stop supporting a seller, commission on that seller stops after 60 days' written notice. Reviewed at 24 months.
5. **Payout** *(Proposal)*: monthly statement by the 10th, and payment by the 15th for fees received in the previous month. Minimum payout amount: *This term requires management approval.*
6. **Currency:** *This term requires management approval* (PKR, or US$ at a stated rate source and date).
7. **Tax:** withholding tax is deducted where the law requires it, and the certificate is provided. Each party handles its own tax registration and returns.
8. **Refunds:** if a seller is refunded, only the commission on that refunded payment is adjusted, shown on the next statement with the reason. Nothing else is touched.
9. **No interest, ever.** Late payments in either direction don't attract interest or percentage penalties. Disputes over late payment go through B15.
10. **Earned commission is protected.** It's never withheld as a penalty, never cancelled retrospectively, and still paid after termination (B14).
11. **Resale pricing** *(resale model only)*: you buy at the wholesale price in your schedule. Solvexo publishes a recommended price. You're free to set your own selling price, but you must show sellers your total price and any service fees in writing before they commit.

### B5. Worked example (illustration only, not a promise)

A Gold partner has **100 sellers on Grow**, paying US$29 a month each (launch price shown on solvexo.store; assumed US$1 = PKR 280).

| Step | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Fees received | 100 × US$29 | US$2,900 ≈ PKR 812,000 |
| Commission (40%) | 2,900 × 40% | **US$1,160 ≈ PKR 324,800 gross a month** |
| Your costs (example) | 2 sales staff, 1 support person, travel, phone, office share | *your own figure*, e.g. PKR 220,000 |

- This is an **example, not a forecast or guarantee**. Your results depend on how many sellers you sign, which plans they need, and how long they stay.
- Commission is earned from real sales to real sellers. **Nothing is paid for recruiting other partners.**

### B6. Territory and exclusivity (Lahore)

**Proposal:**

| Phase | Period | Status | Milestones to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | First 90 days | Authorised Reseller, **non-exclusive** in Lahore | X paying sellers, Y stores live with 5+ products, complaint rate under Z% |

*X, Y, Z and the quarterly milestones: This term requires management approval.*

**What exclusivity covers and doesn't cover:**
- Solvexo won't appoint another reseller in Lahore during the exclusive period.
- **Sellers who sign up directly** on solvexo.store from Lahore remain free to do so. Attribution follows the rules in B7. Solvexo may assign them to you for support, with commission as set in your schedule.
- **House accounts:** sellers who were already Solvexo customers before your agreement, or national chains Solvexo handles directly, are listed in your schedule and excluded.
- **Missing milestones** in one quarter triggers a meeting and a 60-day improvement plan. Missing them again may end exclusivity (not the partnership) with 30 days' notice. Commission already earned and sellers you already signed stay yours.

### B7. Deal registration and attribution

1. Register a prospect in the partner system (for now, the shared pipeline tracker) **with the seller's permission**.
2. Solvexo accepts or rejects within 2 working days *(Proposal)*. A rejection gives a reason, e.g. already a customer or registered by someone else.
3. An accepted registration is protected for **90 days** *(Proposal)*. It's extended if you show active progress (demo held, trial started).
4. A sale is attributed to you through your **partner link or code**, or your accepted registration, whichever is earlier and evidenced.
5. **Duplicate claims:** the earliest accepted, evidenced registration wins. If two partners genuinely worked together, the split is agreed in writing before the sale.
6. Solvexo will **not go around you** to avoid paying commission on an accepted deal.
7. **The seller always chooses.** Registration never stops a seller choosing another partner or buying directly.

### B9. Honest selling: conduct standards

**Always:**
- Use only claims on the **Approved Claims** list (`product/approved-claims.md`) and current prices from solvexo.store.
- Give the seller in writing, before they commit: plan, features, monthly price, renewal, the 7-day trial, cancellation, and who supports them.
- Say clearly that you're an authorised Solvexo partner, not Solvexo itself.
- Recommend the plan that fits the seller, not the one that pays you more.

**Never:**
- Promise sales, income or growth, or features that aren't live (e.g. a native mobile POS app, which is on the roadmap).
- Use fake reviews, fake urgency ("offer ends today" when it doesn't) or misleading comparisons with other platforms.
- Give or take bribes, kickbacks or secret payments.
- Use religious sentiment to pressure a buyer, or call Solvexo "Shariah-certified".
- Discourage a seller from cancelling, complaining or asking for a refund.
- Send bulk WhatsApp messages or use bought contact lists.
- Appoint sub-agents without Solvexo's written approval. Any approved sub-agent must follow these same rules.

### B10. Customer protection

- Sellers can cancel or change plan anytime (as published on solvexo.store).
- Refund requests go to Solvexo within 2 working days *(Proposal)*. The partner never decides refunds alone.
- If the partnership ends, sellers keep their stores and data and are moved to Solvexo or another partner without interruption.
- Each seller's data belongs to that seller. Partners only see what they need to support them.

### B11. Data, confidentiality and brand

- Use seller data **only** to sell and support Solvexo. Don't share it, sell it, or put it into public AI tools.
- Get admin access to a seller's store only with the seller's written permission, and give it up when no longer needed.
- Report any data incident to Solvexo within 24 hours *(Proposal)*.
- Use the Solvexo name and logo only as in the brand kit (`brand/`). Write "Authorised Solvexo Partner", never "Solvexo Lahore" or similar.
- Don't register domains, social accounts or business names containing "Solvexo".
- Pricing, roadmap and internal materials are confidential. This survives the agreement for 2 years *(Proposal)*.

- **Monthly:** pipeline review (shared tracker): prospects, demos, stores live, paying sellers, open support issues.
- **Quarterly business review:** results vs milestones, retention, complaints, next quarter's plan.
- **What's measured:** quality as well as volume: paying sellers, sellers still paying after 3 months, time to first sale, support response time, complaints.

### B14. Term, termination and exit

- **Term:** 12 months, renewing automatically unless either side gives 60 days' notice *(Proposal)*. The pilot is 90 days.
- **Ending for a fixable breach:** written notice, then 30 days to fix it.
- **Immediate suspension** only for serious cases: fraud, data misuse, bribery, or misrepresenting Solvexo. The reason must be given in writing.
- **On exit:**
  - commission earned up to the end date is paid on the normal schedule
  - commission on existing sellers after exit: *This term requires management approval*
  - prepaid amounts for services not delivered are returned
  - sellers are handed over in an orderly way, and seller data is returned or deleted
  - brand use stops

### B15. Disputes

1. Discussion between the named contacts within 14 days.
2. Escalation to the owners/directors within a further 14 days.
3. Mediation by a mutually agreed mediator.
4. If still unresolved: arbitration in Lahore *(Proposal)*, under the governing law in the signed agreement.

Liability is limited to proven direct losses. No arbitrary fines.

### C2. Islamic alignment

The programme is built on these principles, applied through specific clauses. **This is not a Shariah certification.** A qualified Shariah reviewer should review the final agreement, especially the resale structure and the payment terms.

| Principle | Source | Where it shows up in this programme |
|---|---|---|
| Fulfil your contracts | Qur'an 5:1: *"O you who believe, fulfil your contracts."* | Written, signed terms; the 40% verbal offer is honoured and defined (A1); earned commission protected (B4.10) |
| Trade by mutual consent, no unjust taking | Qur'an 4:29: *"…do not consume one another's wealth unjustly, but only by trade with mutual consent."* | Clear commission base; no hidden fees; the seller sees the total price before committing (B4.11, B9) |
| No interest (*riba*) | Qur'an 2:275 | No interest or percentage penalties on late payments (B4.9) |
| No excessive uncertainty (*gharar*) | Prohibition of *bayʿ al-gharar*, Sahih Muslim 1513 | Every amount, timing and condition defined; values not yet decided are flagged, not left vague |
| Truthfulness, no deception | "Whoever deceives is not of us," Sahih Muslim 102 | Approved claims only; no fake urgency or reviews (B9) |
| No bribery (*rishwah*) | Sunan Abi Dawud 3580 | No kickbacks or secret payments (B9) |
| Pay what is earned on time | "Give the worker his wages before his sweat dries," Sunan Ibn Majah 2443 | Fixed monthly payout date (B4.5) |
| Trust (*amānah*) | Qur'an 4:58 | Protection of sellers' money and data (B10, B11) |
| Earnings from real work | | Commission only on real sales; nothing for recruiting other partners (B5) |

**Open Shariah questions:**
1. In the resale model, Solvexo sells a *right to use a service*, not ownership. The reviewer should confirm the structure.
2. Converting PKR and US$: the rate and date must be fixed and agreed, so there's no uncertainty.
3. **Prohibited goods:** Solvexo needs a published policy that stores may not sell items that are illegal in Pakistan or clearly impermissible (e.g. alcohol, gambling). Partners should not knowingly onboard such sellers.
