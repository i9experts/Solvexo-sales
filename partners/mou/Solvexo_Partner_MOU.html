1Parties

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is made on between:

  1. , a company registered under the laws of , with its registered office at , operating the Solvexo commerce platform ("Solvexo"); and
  2. , a (company / firm / sole proprietor / individual) registered under the laws of , with its registered office at ("Partner").

Solvexo and the Partner are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

2Background

  1. Solvexo provides a connected commerce platform that allows sellers to build an online store, sell in person with a point of sale (POS), and manage products, inventory, orders, customers and analytics in one place (the "Platform").
  2. The Partner has experience, relationships and capacity in and wishes to introduce, sell and/or support the Platform for sellers.
  3. The Parties wish to record the principles on which they intend to work together under the Solvexo Channel Partner Programme (the "Programme"), before entering into a definitive Partner Agreement.

3Purpose and nature of this MOU

  1. This MOU sets out the Parties' shared understanding of the partner tier, partnership model, commercial principles and responsibilities that will form the basis of a definitive Partner Agreement and Commercial Schedule (together, the "Definitive Agreement").
  2. The Parties intend to negotiate in good faith and sign the Definitive Agreement within days of this MOU.
  3. Except for clauses 13 (Confidentiality), 14 (Data protection), 15 (Intellectual property), 16.3–16.4, 17 (Costs), 18 (Governing law and disputes) and 19 (General provisions), which are binding, this MOU is not a legally binding contract and creates no obligation to enter into the Definitive Agreement.
  4. No commission, margin, exclusivity or other commercial right arises under this MOU. These arise only under the signed Definitive Agreement. Where the Parties agree in writing to begin a pilot before the Definitive Agreement is signed, the pilot terms will be recorded in a signed pilot letter.

6Commercial principles

The Parties intend the Definitive Agreement to reflect the following principles:

  1. Commission base. Commission is calculated on subscription fees actually received by Solvexo from sellers attributed to the Partner (or, in the resale model, on the wholesale amount paid by the Partner), excluding sales tax/VAT, payment processing fees, refunds and chargebacks.
  2. Earning event. Commission is earned when the seller's payment is received, not at sign-up or during a free trial.
  3. Rates. The commission rate or wholesale discount for each tier is set out in Annex A and confirmed in the Commercial Schedule.
  4. Statements and payment. Solvexo will issue a monthly statement by the day of each month and pay by the day, for fees received in the previous month, in (currency).
  5. Taxes. Each Party is responsible for its own taxes. Withholding tax will be deducted where required by law and a certificate provided.
  6. Refunds. If a seller is refunded, only the commission on that refunded payment is adjusted, shown on the next statement with the reason.
  7. No interest. No interest, mark-up or percentage-based penalty will apply to any late payment by either Party.
  8. Earned commission is protected. Commission earned is never withheld as a penalty, cancelled retrospectively or forfeited on termination.
  9. Resale pricing (resale model only). Solvexo publishes a recommended price. The Partner sets its own selling price but must disclose the total price and any service fees to the seller in writing before the seller commits.
  10. No guaranteed income. Earnings depend entirely on genuine sales to sellers. No payment is made for recruiting other partners.

7Territory and exclusivity

  1. Registered, Silver and Gold Partners are non-exclusive. They may operate in the territory described in Annex D.
  2. A Regional Partner may be granted exclusivity as Solvexo's only appointed reseller in the defined region, for a defined period, conditional on meeting the milestones in Annex D. Exclusivity normally follows a -day non-exclusive pilot.
  3. Exclusivity does not prevent sellers from signing up directly with Solvexo, and does not cover the house accounts listed in Annex D.
  4. If milestones are missed, the Parties will meet and agree a 60-day improvement plan. Exclusivity (not the partnership) may end on 30 days' written notice if milestones are missed again. Sellers already signed and commission already earned remain with the Partner.

9Deal registration and attribution

  1. The Partner registers prospects through Solvexo's approved system, with the prospect's permission.
  2. Solvexo accepts or rejects a registration within working days, giving a reason for any rejection. An accepted registration is protected for days, extendable on evidence of active progress.
  3. A sale is attributed through the Partner's link/code or accepted registration, whichever is earlier and evidenced. Where two partners worked together, the split is agreed in writing before the sale.
  4. Solvexo will not bypass an accepted registration to avoid commission. The seller always remains free to choose its supplier.

10Brand, marketing and claims

  1. The Partner will describe itself as an "Authorised Solvexo Partner" and will not present itself as Solvexo, its employee, or its exclusive distributor (unless appointed Regional Partner).
  2. The Partner will use only the official Solvexo brand kit, current prices from solvexo.store, and claims on Solvexo's approved claims list.
  3. The Partner will not promise features that are not live, guaranteed sales or income, or any discount, delivery date or custom work not approved by Solvexo in writing.
  4. The Partner will not register any domain, social media account or business name containing "Solvexo".

11Ethical conduct

  1. Both Parties will act honestly, fulfil their commitments, and avoid deception, bribery, interest-based charges and unclear terms, in line with the Partner Code of Conduct in Annex B.
  2. Both Parties will comply with all applicable laws, including those on advertising, consumer protection, competition, tax, anti-corruption, electronic transactions and data protection.
  3. The Partner will not appoint sub-agents without Solvexo's written approval. Any approved sub-agent must follow the same obligations.
  4. Neither Party will describe this arrangement as "Shariah-certified" or "Shariah-compliant" unless a qualified Shariah reviewer has issued a written opinion on the final agreement.

12Customer protection

  1. Before a seller commits, the Partner will give the seller, in writing: the plan, features, total monthly price, renewal terms, free-trial terms, cancellation rights and who provides support.
  2. Refund requests are passed to Solvexo promptly. The Partner will never discourage a legitimate complaint, cancellation or refund.
  3. If the partnership ends, sellers keep their stores and data and are transitioned to Solvexo or another partner without interruption.

13Confidentiality Binding

  1. Each Party will keep confidential all non-public information received from the other, including pricing, commercial terms, roadmap, seller lists and business plans, and use it only for the purpose of this MOU.
  2. This does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the receiving Party, already lawfully known to it, or required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice where lawful).
  3. This clause survives for 2 years after this MOU ends.

14Data protection Binding

  1. Each seller's store data belongs to that seller. The Partner will access a seller's store only with the seller's written permission, and only as needed to support them.
  2. Personal data will be used only for the Programme, kept secure, never sold, and never entered into public AI tools.
  3. Each Party will notify the other of any data incident affecting the other's data within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.

15Intellectual property Binding

  1. Solvexo owns all rights in the Platform, the Solvexo name, logo and materials. The Partner receives a limited, revocable, non-transferable right to use the brand kit solely to promote the Platform during the Programme.
  2. The Partner owns its own name, brand and materials. Nothing in this MOU transfers ownership of either Party's intellectual property.

16Term, review and termination

  1. This MOU starts on the date of the last signature and continues until the earlier of: (a) signature of the Definitive Agreement; or (b) months from that date.
  2. The Parties will review progress together within days of signature.
  3. Either Party may end this MOU by 30 days' written notice, without liability, except for breach of a binding clause.
  4. On expiry or termination, each Party will return or destroy the other's confidential information on request, and the Partner will stop using the Solvexo brand. Binding clauses survive as stated.

17Costs Binding

Each Party bears its own costs in connection with this MOU and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreement. No fee is payable by the Partner to join the Programme.

18Governing law and disputes Binding

  1. This MOU is governed by the laws of .
  2. The Parties will first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion between named contacts (14 days), then between senior management (a further 14 days), then by mediation.
  3. If unresolved, the dispute will be referred to arbitration seated in , before a sole arbitrator agreed by the Parties, under the applicable arbitration law. Liability is limited to proven direct loss. No arbitrary fines or penalties apply.

19General provisions Binding

  1. Relationship. Nothing in this MOU creates a partnership in law, joint venture, employment or agency, or authorises either Party to bind the other.
  2. Entire understanding. This MOU, with its Annexes, is the Parties' entire understanding on its subject and replaces any earlier verbal or written discussions.
  3. Changes. Any change must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
  4. Notices. Notices are given in writing to the addresses in clause 1, or by email to the contacts in Annex C.
  5. Counterparts and e-signature. This MOU may be signed in counterparts and electronically, each of which is an original.

Annex B

Partner Code of Conduct

Always

  • Use only approved claims and current published prices
  • Give sellers the full offer in writing before they commit
  • Identify yourself as an Authorised Solvexo Partner
  • Recommend the plan that fits the seller's needs
  • Protect sellers' money, data and passwords
  • Pass on complaints and refund requests promptly
  • Keep accurate records of sales and collections

Never

  • Promise sales, income, growth or unreleased features
  • Use fake reviews, fake urgency or misleading comparisons
  • Offer or accept bribes, kickbacks or secret payments
  • Use religious sentiment to pressure a buyer
  • Send bulk messages or use purchased contact lists
  • Discourage a cancellation, complaint or refund
  • Onboard sellers of goods that are illegal or clearly impermissible
