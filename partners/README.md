# Partner / Reseller Programme

**Status: proposed framework only. No Solvexo product schedule is approved.**

The i9 Experts partner framework has a common set of terms for **individual agents** and **SME channel partners**, plus a separate product schedule for each product. The Solvexo schedule can't be drafted until these are confirmed:

- Exact live functions and customer types
- Pricing basis (subscription, commission, both)
- What data Solvexo handles for sellers
- Implementation and support requirements

## Until then

- Don't promise any commission rate, protection period, territory, exclusivity or payout date. The answer is: *"This term requires management approval."*
- Don't reuse Eldermin, SmartVan or other product terms for Solvexo.
- Partner materials must be marked *"Proposed terms — subject to management approval."*
