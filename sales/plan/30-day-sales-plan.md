# Solvexo: 30-Day Sales Plan

**Period:** Mon 23 Nov – Tue 22 Dec 2026
**Team:** 3–5 sales people / interns + founder
**Budget:** PKR 100k (lean) to PKR 180k (full)

## Goal

**40 seller stores live by 22 Dec 2026, each with at least 5 real products, and 10 of them with their first real order.**

| Segment | Stores live | Why this many |
|---|---|---|
| Karachi shops with a counter | 25 | Field visits convert best, and POS + store is our strongest demo |
| Instagram / WhatsApp sellers (Pakistan) | 12 | Cheap to reach online, quick to set up |
| Gulf sellers (test) | 3 | Prove sign-up and payments work outside Pakistan before spending more |

These targets are **planning assumptions** (visit → demo about 30%, demo → live store about 25%). Replace them with real rates after week 2.

## The bottleneck

Solvexo has **no proof yet**: no live sellers to show and no reviews. Shop owners in Karachi buy from what their neighbours use. So the first 10 live sellers matter more than anything else. Get them live, make them succeed, then use them (with permission) to sell the next 30.

## Positioning (STP)

- **Segment:** small and medium sellers who sell in more than one place (a counter and Instagram/WhatsApp) and lose track of stock and orders.
- **Target (first 30 days):** single-shop owners in Karachi markets with 100–1,000 products, plus active Instagram sellers who already get orders in DMs.
- **Position:** one app for the counter, the online store and the stock.

**Message**
- English: *Your shop counter and your online store, one app, one stock count.*
- Urdu: *دکان کی سیل اور آن لائن آرڈر — ایک ہی ایپ، ایک ہی اسٹاک۔*

## Fix in week 1 before selling hard

These block the field team from answering basic questions:

1. **Pricing and "free":** what exactly is free, and what do sellers pay later? Add it to `pricing/` with a date.
2. **Payments and payouts:** which methods work for Pakistani sellers (e.g. bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa, COD) and for Gulf sellers? When does the seller get the money?
3. **Setup claim:** is it "3-minute setup" or "account ready within 24 hours"? Pick one for the flyer and the pitch.
4. **Founding Seller benefits:** only offer what the team can really deliver (e.g. free product upload help).
5. **Support:** one WhatsApp number for seller support, and who answers it.

## Stop / don't do

- Don't advertise to **buyers** yet. Sellers first, buyers once there is inventory.
- Don't send bulk WhatsApp messages or buy contact lists. It gets the number banned.
- Don't count sign-ups as wins. A store only counts once it has 5+ real products.

## Open questions

- Is there a commission or transaction fee on orders? (This affects the pitch to price-sensitive shops.)
- Which Gulf country first, UAE or KSA? It decides currency, payments and ad targeting.
- Can sales reps get a demo account each?
