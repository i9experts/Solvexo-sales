# Sales Process

The stages match the columns in `trackers/Solvexo_Seller_Pipeline_Tracker.xlsx`. Fill in each date when the stage happens.

| # | Stage | What "done" means | Your next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First contact** | Spoke to the owner or decision maker (not just staff) | Book a demo within 3 days |
| 2 | **Demo booked** | Date and time agreed | Send a WhatsApp reminder the day before |
| 3 | **Demo held** | Showed POS + store on their own products | Help them sign up in the same sitting if possible |
| 4 | **Store live** | Store link works with at least 5 real products | Follow up after 7 days: first order yet? |
| 5 | **Paid / first sale** | On a paid plan, or first real sale made | Ask for a referral and, with permission, a short review |

**Rule:** every open lead has a *Next action* and a *Next action date*. A lead without one is a lost lead.

## Qualify in 4 questions

1. **What do you sell, and where?** (shop, Instagram, WhatsApp, marketplace)
2. **How do you track stock and orders today?** (register, Excel, another app)
3. **Who decides?** (owner, partner, manager)
4. **What's bothering you most?** (stock mistakes, missed orders, no online sales, cash counting)

A good lead has products ready to sell, a stock or order problem they feel, and the decision maker in the room.

## Where to find sellers

| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| Market visits | Walk a market (mobile accessories, clothing, cosmetics). Use the flyer and QR code. Best before shop rush hours. |
| Instagram / Facebook sellers | Reply to their posts or send a polite DM. One message, no spam. |
| Referrals | Ask every live seller for two names. |
| Trade associations / market committees | Offer a group demo session |
| WhatsApp | Only to people who have shared their number with you. No bought lists. |

## Weekly numbers to report

1. New first contacts
2. Demos held
3. Stores live
4. Paid sellers / first sales
5. Overdue follow-ups (should be zero)
