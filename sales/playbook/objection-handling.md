# Objection Handling

Answer honestly. If the honest answer is "I don't know yet", say so and promise a date to come back.

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already sell on Instagram / WhatsApp, it's working." | "Keep doing that. Solvexo gives you a store link to put in your bio, so orders come in one place instead of scattered DMs." |
| "I'm on Daraz already." | "Daraz is their marketplace. Solvexo gives you your own store and your own customer list, plus POS for the shop. Many sellers do both." *(Don't criticise the competitor.)* |
| "I'm not good with technology." | "If you use WhatsApp, you can use this. Let's add your first product together right now." |
| "How much does it cost?" | "First 7 days are free, no card needed. With the launch offer it's then US$1 a month for 3 months, then from US$6 a month on Basic. And no commission on cash on delivery or shop sales." *(Check the offer is still on the [pricing page](https://www.solvexo.store/pricing).)* |
| "Do you take a cut of my sales?" | "No. 0% on cash on delivery, bank transfer and in-store sales. Card payments through Solvexo Payments have no commission, only the normal card fee." |
| "Dollars? I don't have a card for that." | **Open issue:** confirm with management how Pakistani shops can pay the subscription before promising anything. Log it as a lost reason "Payment / payout concerns" if it blocks the sale. |
| "How will I get paid for online orders?" | Cash on delivery and bank transfer go straight to you. For card payouts in Pakistan, **confirm** with management first. Never guess. |
| "I don't have time right now." | "No problem. When's your quiet hour? I'll come back then, it takes 15 minutes." *(Log the next-action date.)* |
| "My data, is it safe?" | "Card details are handled by Stripe. Solvexo never stores card numbers. Your orders, customers and stock are only visible to your account." (From the [Security page](https://www.solvexo.store/security).) |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What would you need to see to decide?" Then book the follow-up. |
