# Live Demo Script (15 minutes)

**Before you go:** use a demo account only, never a real seller's store. Charge your phone and laptop. Check that the demo store loads.

| Minutes | Do this | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Ask the 4 qualifying questions (see `../playbook/sales-process.md`) | "Before I show anything, how do you handle stock and orders today?" |
| 2–5 | **POS sale:** ring up an item, apply a discount, take a split payment, show the receipt | "This is your counter. Cash, discount, receipt, all done." |
| 5–7 | **Stock sync:** open the online store and show the same item's stock has dropped | "You sold one in the shop, and the online stock updated by itself." |
| 7–10 | **Online store:** open the storefront on their phone and place a test order | "This is your own link. Put it in your Instagram bio." |
| 10–12 | **Dashboard / analytics:** show the order arriving and the sales overview | "Every order, from the counter or online, lands here." |
| 12–15 | **Close:** sign up together and add their first real product | "Let's put your first product live right now." |

**Show only features you've seen working on the demo account.** If something breaks, say "let me show you that on my next visit". Don't improvise.

**After the demo:** update the tracker (Demo held date, Next action, Next action date) the same day.
